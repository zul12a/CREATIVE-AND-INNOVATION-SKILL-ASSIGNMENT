05 — Benefits of the Solutions

Who Gains, and How

The five solutions were chosen because they benefit both sides of the relationship — the customer discovering products, and the brand building loyalty.

For Customers

  • The Skin Quiz and Find My Product feature make it easier to identify suitable products, reducing time spent browsing a large catalogue.
  • The Smart Skincare Routine Builder helps beginners understand how products fit into a daily routine.
  • The Interactive Ingredient Guide improves understanding of skincare ingredients and reduces confusion when comparing products.
  • Personalised Bundles make shopping more convenient by presenting related products together.

For Cosmoderm

  • These features encourage customers to explore more of the brand's existing product portfolio.
  • A personalised experience increases engagement, since customers receive information relevant to their individual needs.
  • The ideas strengthen Cosmoderm's digital presence, turning the website into more than a product catalogue.
  • The website becomes an interactive platform where customers learn, identify needs, and discover suitable products in one place.
