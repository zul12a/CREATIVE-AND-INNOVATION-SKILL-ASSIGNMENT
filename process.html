03 — Process of Identifying the Solutions

From Observation to Concept

Each proposed idea in this case study followed the same path — grounded in the real customer journey, not introduced for its own sake.

How the Solutions Were Developed

The process began with an observation of Cosmoderm's online product catalogue and the range of products available. The sheer variety showed that customers have plenty of choice — but also highlighted potential difficulty in deciding where to begin.

From there, the next step was identifying the main user needs: clear information, simple product recommendations, easy navigation, and guidance that doesn't require advanced skincare knowledge. Several ideas were then generated through brainstorming, and compared based on usefulness, simplicity, and their potential to improve the customer experience.

This process ensured that each proposed innovation addresses a specific customer problem, rather than introducing a new feature without a clear purpose.

The Five Focus Areas

Product Discovery → Personalisation → Education → Routine Building → Interactive Shopping

These five areas became the foundation for the five creative solutions covered next — each one mapped directly back to a problem identified in the previous section.
